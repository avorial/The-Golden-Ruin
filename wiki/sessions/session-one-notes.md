# Session One Notes

## Arrival at the House of the North Star

The characters arrived at the **[[House of the North Star]]**, where **[[Silas Burke, Head Butler|Silas Burke]]** welcomed them. Burke explained the house rules, introduced the staff, and allowed everyone to choose their rooms.

During their first exploration of the house, several characters made unusual discoveries.

### Marcus

While wandering through the house, Marcus discovered a small collection of **gears made from a strange, unfamiliar metal**. He took possession of them.

### Edward

While exploring the grounds, Edward discovered a strange **pig pen**. One pig simply stood and stared at him in an unnerving manner.

Inside the feeding trough, Edward found a **bloodstained lumberjack shirt**. He secretly took the shirt back to the house, cleaned it, and hid it among his belongings.

### Jackson

Jackson began studying occult books available at the North Star. He became particularly interested in **wards and protective symbols**, including wards associated with keeping out or restraining the undead.

This knowledge became important later during the gathering at Bonnet's house.

## House Staff

### [[Silas Burke, Head Butler|Silas Burke]]

**Head Butler and House Manager.** Formal, controlled, and extremely observant. Burke seems to know nearly everything that enters or leaves the North Star.

### [[Élise Marceau, Maid|Élise Marceau]]

**Maid.** Friendly and observant. She notices emotional details and interpersonal tensions others overlook.

### [[Madame Eugénie Voclain, Head Cook|Madame Eugénie Voclain]]

**Cook and Housekeeper.** Madame Voclain knew Nell Whitcomb during Nell's time at the North Star and remembers her fondly.

### [[Étienne Renaud, Valet|Étienne Renaud]]

**Valet and Footman.** Renaud handles luggage, messages, errands, visitors, and dealings with outside servants.

## The Sculpture Unveiling

The group attended a private sculpture unveiling at [[Hôtel Bonnet]], the home of **[[Dr. Georges Bonnet]]**. The centerpiece of the evening was Bonnet's sculpture of **[[Eleanor “Nell” Whitcomb]]**, a former North Star resident.

### [[Dr. Georges Bonnet]]

**Physician, sculptor, and host.**

During the evening, Jackson accidentally disturbed or broke what appeared to be an occult **ward** while moving a piece of furniture. Immediately afterward, Bonnet's hand briefly turned an unnatural **yellow color**. Bonnet concealed it and attempted to continue as though nothing had happened.

The incident suggested that the ward might somehow have been preserving Bonnet or keeping something dead alive.

Bonnet had been romantically involved with Nell. He also had an earlier relationship with [[Janine Du Bois]] while they were in Venice. Although that relationship ended, emotional tension remains between them. Bonnet possesses a **hidden sculpture of Janine** not included in the public exhibition.

Bonnet also knows [[Dr. Ludwig Weiss]] from many years earlier. Their conversation suggested that Weiss might once have been Bonnet's medical teacher or mentor.

### [[Eleanor “Nell” Whitcomb]]

**Twenty-four-year-old American artist, dancer, and former North Star resident.**

Nell was the model for Bonnet's new sculpture. She recognized the name of **Edward's patron** and apparently knew him.

During the evening, Nell invited the group to meet her later at a restaurant near the North Star. She gave Edward a small drawing made on the back of a calling card belonging to **[[Louis Lépine]]**, Prefect of Police.

Nell also spoke about another former North Star resident named **Roberts**, a sculptor currently in **Florence** whose family made its fortune in the **Canadian lumber business**.

At the end of the session, **[[Arsène Lupin]] arrived and announced that Nell Whitcomb was dead.**

### [[Dr. Ludwig Weiss]]

**Elderly Austrian physician famous for experimental surgery.**

Weiss said he had known Bonnet for many years and that they had worked together before. Their history appeared old enough that Weiss might once have served as Bonnet's medical teacher or mentor.

Weiss had been expected in Paris **six weeks earlier**, but his arrival was delayed by a stroke.

### [[Janine Du Bois]]

**Thirty-two-year-old soprano and Bonnet's former lover.**

Janine and Bonnet had been romantically involved years earlier, particularly during time spent together in **Venice**. Although they are no longer together, a strong connection remains.

Janine performed during the evening. Her singing carried unnaturally through the surrounding streets, reaching much farther than it reasonably should have. Bonnet secretly possesses an old sculpture of her.

### [[Louis Lépine]]

**Prefect of Police.**

Nell used Lépine's calling card for the drawing she gave Edward. Following Nell's death, Lépine became involved in the investigation.

### Adrien Mercier

**Thirty-eight-year-old sculptor.**

Mercier studied Bonnet's work and noticed that his sculpting techniques seemed generations out of date, resembling methods from a much earlier period.

### Théo Marchand

**Thirty-five-year-old painter and gossip.**

Marchand had heard strange rumors about Bonnet's career and suggested there were inconsistencies in his history and artistic development.

### Camille Roussel

**Forty-six-year-old art critic.**

Roussel was familiar with Bonnet's earlier work. He said the hidden sculpture of Janine was supposedly created about ten years ago, but Bonnet simply **was not skilled enough at that time to have produced a work of such quality**. Judged against Bonnet's known career, the sculpture should have been impossible for him to create.

## Major Clues Established

- Marcus possesses strange metal gears of unknown origin.
- Edward possesses the bloodstained lumberjack shirt.
- A pig at the North Star behaves strangely.
- Jackson has begun learning about occult wards.
- Breaking or disturbing a ward appears to affect Bonnet physically.
- Bonnet's hand briefly turned yellow.
- Bonnet may be unnaturally preserved or connected to death.
- Bonnet's sculpting methods appear much older than they should be.
- The Janine sculpture appears to predate Bonnet's ability to create it.
- Bonnet and Weiss have a long and possibly medical relationship.
- Bonnet had relationships with both Janine and Nell.
- Janine's voice appears to possess an unnatural quality.
- Nell knew Edward's patron.
- Nell gave Edward a drawing on Louis Lépine's calling card.
- Roberts, another North Star sculptor, is currently in Florence.
- Roberts comes from a Canadian lumber family.
- Arsène Lupin announced that **Nell Whitcomb is dead**.

## Session Ending

The evening concluded with the sudden arrival of **[[Arsène Lupin]]**. His announcement cut through the aftermath of the gathering:

> **Nell Whitcomb is dead.**

Session One ended there.
