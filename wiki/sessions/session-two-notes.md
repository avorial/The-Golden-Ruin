# Session Two Notes

## The Immediate Case

[[Eleanor “Nell” Whitcomb]] is confirmed dead. [[Louis Lépine]] reports that she suffered many stab wounds and that organs are missing. Later details suggest that what was taken may be stranger than ordinary organ removal. Lépine notes that, although many people die in Paris, deaths among foreigners are uncommon. He also remarks on the number of unusual incidents involving people connected to the [[House of the North Star]].

Lépine gives the investigators his card. [[Jackson-Jay-Steerman-IV-David|Jay]] has a copy of Lépine's card bearing an image on the back, which he had intended to discuss with Nell. Jay also says that Roberts has not been seen in Florence, despite the party being told that he had been there for months.

## Nell's Apartment

The investigators, including [[Laurie Shelby|Laurie]] and [[Jackson-Jay-Steerman-IV-David|Jay]], visit Nell's apartment in the [[7th Arrondissement]]. An ambulance waits outside.

Nell's roommate, Marie Marehan, has survived a suicide attempt. An ambulance medic suggests that Nell and Marie were lovers, but Jay does not believe this.

Marie says that, at the moment of Nell's death, she felt the parts missing from Nell's body. She describes "something removed," which may not mean organs. She continues to feel Nell's body within her own and is taken to an asylum.

Under Marie's bed, the investigators find a two-shot derringer with one shot fired. They also find a picture showing her connection to the characters, a stack of newspapers about incidents involving people connected to the North Star, and a jar containing an unidentified chemical.

## The Wall-Poem

The investigators discover a poem on the wallpaper. Removing the wallpaper reveals that the words are also burned into the wall beneath it. They take the wallpaper bearing the poem.

While one investigator copies the poem, the written copy finishes itself. The investigator does not see this happen; they glance down and find the transcription complete.

![The wall-poem written in dark red lettering on worn floral wallpaper.](/wiki/media/Session-Two-Wall-Poem.png)

The poem reads as follows, preserving the wording shown in the image:

> The dying twilight of the twin suns\
> Embrases and irises the sky with tattered reds.\
> Nothing warms more than the purple sunset\
> When I dread the cold of the declining night.\
> But even in the ruddy hour, the flames of the heavens\
> Are poor marvels in front of the hair\
> of Camilla.

## Janine Du Bois

The investigators visit [[Janine Du Bois]] at her home for high tea. She is aware of the day's news.

Janine recalls attending a party ten years earlier at which three people died. [[Dr. Ludwig Weiss]] was present, while [[Dr. Georges Bonnet]] had left the day before.

Janine will be appearing in a new opera and offers to arrange tickets for the investigators. The group plans to have dinner with her. Later, she leaves for an after-party with "old friends," but gets into a carriage alone.

## Marie Curie

At the restaurant, the investigators meet [[Marie Curie]]. Like the party, she has lost members of her group. She explains that she once had a team of researchers, but they are being removed from the timeline over time.

Curie says that she has developed a chemical that allows her to enter an "in-between realm." She also believes that radium allows people to see where those from another timeline once were.

## Other Leads

- **[[Edward-Vine-Jarod|Edward Vine]]:** The party knows Edward is gone, but the staff has no memory of him. The investigators keep remembering the time Edward spent there.
- **The captain's tapestry:** In the captain's room, an unusual tapestry depicts natives "fighting" buffalo. It changes while he sleeps, and he pushes it out of the room.
- **Roberts:** A former [[House of the North Star]] member from a logging family, Roberts is believed to be missing in Florence. The shirt found in the pig trough may be connected to him.
- **[[Henry-Pirece-Laura|Henry Pierce]]:** Henry stays behind to write a character piece about [[Dr. Ludwig Weiss]] and his unusual association with [[Dr. Georges Bonnet]].
- **[[Henri-de-Toulouse-Lautrec|Toulouse-Lautrec]]:** He appears, along with a man wearing an eccentric scarf.
- **The newspaper collection:** Someone has been tracking mysterious incidents involving people connected to the North Star.

## Major Clues

- Nell suffered many stab wounds, and organs or other parts of her body are missing.
- Marie felt the missing parts at the moment of Nell's death and still feels Nell's body within her own.
- A two-shot derringer beneath Marie's bed has been fired once.
- The apartment contains a picture connecting Marie to the characters, newspaper clippings about North Star incidents, and an unidentified chemical.
- The wall-poem remains burned into the wall after the wallpaper is removed.
- A copy of the poem completed itself while being transcribed.
- Janine attended a party ten years ago where three people died; Weiss was present, and Bonnet had left the previous day.
- Curie's researchers are being removed from the timeline.
- Curie claims that a chemical permits entry into an in-between realm and that radium can reveal traces of people from another timeline.
- Roberts has not been seen in Florence, contradicting what the party was told.
