# Sacré-Cœur

## Overview

Construction began in 1874 on the hill where rebels were massacred. In 1895 it remains incomplete and politically charged, a religious monument to an unhealed civic wound.

## Investigation Use

Workmen uncover a communal burial beneath a foundation meant to deny the dead.
