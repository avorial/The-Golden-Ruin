# Pyotr Rachkovsky

<div class="wiki-layout">
<div class="wiki-main">

<section class="wiki-section">
<h2>At The Table</h2>
<p>Rachkovsky is courteous, patient, and almost impossible to read. He never seems surprised, which may mean he knows everything, or that he has practiced the expression.</p>
</section>

<section class="wiki-section">
<h2>History</h2>
<p>Rachkovsky operates in Paris for Russian state security, monitoring radicals, manipulating informants, and shaping narratives useful to the regime. He treats espionage as authorship with consequences.</p>
</section>

<section class="wiki-section">
<h2>Campaign Use</h2>
<p>He is ideal for spy plots, forged documents, provocateurs, and political pressure. Rachkovsky can be patron, antagonist, or both within the same conversation.</p>
</section>

## Hidden Truth

Rachkovsky has encountered Carcosan influence inside a manufactured conspiracy. He does not know whether he invented a doorway or merely found one.

## GM Ideas

- He hires the characters to investigate a cult he first dismissed as useful propaganda.
- An informant's report contains a phrase from *The King in Yellow*.
- Rachkovsky frames an occult crime as revolutionary violence to protect his operation.
:::
