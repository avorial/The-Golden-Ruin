# Les Deux Magots

## Overview

Its name comes from the wooden figures above the drinkers, though occult patrons may interpret them as magicians or alchemists. Mallarmé and other Symbolist poets frequent the café.

## Investigation Use

One figure changes posture between visits, pointing toward a patron who has encountered the Yellow Sign.
