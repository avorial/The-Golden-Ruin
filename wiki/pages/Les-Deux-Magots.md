# Les Deux Magots

## Overview

Its name comes from the wooden figures above the drinkers, though occult patrons may interpret them as magicians or alchemists. Mallarmé and other Symbolist poets frequent the café.

## Historical Background

Les Deux Magots (French pronunciation: [le dø maɡo]) is a café and restaurant situated at 6, Place Saint-Germain-des-Prés in Paris' 6th arrondissement, France. It once had a reputation as the main rendezvous of the literary and intellectual elite of the city. It is now a popular tourist destination.

This background is adapted from the [Les Deux Magots](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Deux_Magots) article on Wikipedia (en), accessed September 23, 2026, under the CC BY-SA license.
