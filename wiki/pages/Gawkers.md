# Gawkers

## Overview

Gawkers materialize wherever a crowd gathers around an accident, scandal, salon sensation, or public spectacle. At a glance they seem ordinary. Seen clearly, their faces are waxy and unfinished. They close around isolated onlookers and erase them into nothing.

## Signs and Investigation

- Photographs show blank-faced figures no witness remembers.
- Attendance counts exceed tickets sold, but one real guest is missing.
- A victim's companions remember arriving with someone yet cannot recall a name.
- Gawkers vanish when observed outside the emotional pressure of a crowd.

## Hidden Truth

They are called across realities by a Carcosan noble or a magician working beneath a black star. Fighting a few does not stop the appearances; the investigators must identify and defeat the summoner.
