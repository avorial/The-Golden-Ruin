# Cabaret du Néant

## Overview

Servers dressed as monks and morticians welcome guests to the Salle de l’Intoxication. Disease-named cocktails arrive in skull cups amid skeletons, gargoyles, and guillotine décor; the “consumption germ” is crème de menthe.

## Historical Background

Cabaret du Néant ([ka.ba.ʁɛ dy ne.ɑ̃], "Cabaret of Nothingness"/"Cabaret of the Void") was a cabaret in Montmartre, Paris, founded in 1892. The Cabaret du Néant was an early pioneer of the modern theme restaurant and dealt with various aspects of mortality and death. The dark theme of the cabaret included magic tricks and illusions focusing on morbid subjects.

This background is adapted from the [Cabaret du Néant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabaret_du_N%C3%A9ant) article on Wikipedia (en), accessed September 23, 2026, under the CC BY-SA license.
