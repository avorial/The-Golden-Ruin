# Session Three Notes

**Played November 9, 2026.** Continues [[Session-Two-Notes|Session Two]].

## At the Pharmacy

[[Marcus-J-rn-Jensen-Yoshi|Járn]] takes the mystery liquid from the investigation of [[Eleanor-Nell-Whitcomb|Nell Whitcomb]]'s apartment to a pharmacist. He hands over one ampoule. The pharmacist says that testing it will consume half the sample. The investigators do not yet have a result.

The notes put the pharmacy four blocks away and appear to give its name as **Voltron** (spelling to confirm).

The pharmacist also has an unclaimed package for Nell: about five ampoules of a white powder called **Hot Dust**, intended to enhance performance. The prescription came from [[Dr-Georges-Bonnet|Dr. Georges Bonnet]]. The powder and the mystery liquid are separate leads; no connection between them has been established.

## The Pig at the North Star

Back at the [[House-of-the-North-Star|House of the North Star]], [[Silas-Burke-Head-Butler|Silas Burke]] tells the investigators that the pig is to be slaughtered. [[Henry-Pirece-Laura|Henry Pierce]] intervenes and stops it. Járn suspects the pig is Roberts, the former North Star associate who knew Nell and was supposedly living in Florence. The investigators' tests strengthen that suspicion, and they find a fine Italian wallet in the same trough where the bloodstained lumberjack shirt turned up in [[Session-One-Notes|Session One]].

The pig came from Gérard, a livestock supplier connected to [[Madame-Eugenie-Voclain-Head-Cook|the cook]]. Gérard's wife is from Florence. Whether the pig was sent to the North Star as some kind of punishment remains an open question.

## Weiss and the Police

The investigators speak with [[Dr-Ludwig-Weiss|Dr. Ludwig Weiss]], then with [[Louis-Lepine|Louis Lépine]] at the police, before returning home. Weiss's passport lists him as **82 years old**.

They also come away with an address for **Paul Tooms**, a writer and journalist who had been at the North Star with Nell and Roberts. Tooms went to Geneva and has been in a sanatorium there for about four weeks. According to the notes, he could sense calamities and wrote them down.

Three other leads remain worth following: [[Adrien-Mercier|Adrien Mercier]], a sculptor familiar with Bonnet; [[Theo-Marchand|Théo Marchand]], who knows about Bonnet's surgery room; and [[Camille-Roussel|Camille Roussel]], an art critic who knew about Bonnet's teacher.

## The Dream in Carcosa

After returning home, the investigators find themselves in Carcosa in a shared dream. There they see Roberts speak in his pig form. What the tests had only suggested is now clear to them: the pig they spared at the North Star is Roberts.

A herd of skinned buffalo stampedes through Carcosa. The King in Yellow rides one of them. The sight recalls the changing buffalo tapestry from the captain's room. The captain takes aim and shoots the King in Yellow. The investigators wake before they see what the shot did.

## Open Questions

- What will the pharmacist find in the mystery liquid? Does it have anything to do with Bonnet's prescription for Hot Dust?
- How did Roberts end up in the North Star's pigpen? Who sent him there, and why?
- What did Paul Tooms write down about the calamities he sensed, and can the investigators speak with him in Geneva?
- What explains the age listed on Weiss's passport?
- What connects the captain's tapestry to the stampede, and what happened after he fired?
