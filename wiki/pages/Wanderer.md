# Wanderer

## Overview

The Wanderer appears as a fashionable flâneur with a skull mask and sword cane. His mesmerizing conversation guides a target through familiar streets that gradually become a quasi-Paris: an endless, Carcosan demi-city from which ordinary routes no longer lead home.

## Investigation and Response

- Missing people were last seen strolling with the same elegant stranger.
- Maps remain recognizable while street names shift toward Hali and Hastur.
- Victims recall long conversations but none of the questions they answered.
- Twilight, alcohol, and drugs make the transition easier.

Rescuers may deliberately follow him into the false city, mark a route independent of its changing signs, and break his conversational rhythm before guiding the lost back.
