# Dépôt de Mendicité

## Overview

It houses about three thousand poor, sick, elderly, and unhoused people, some voluntary and some sentenced for vagrancy. Hat or ribbon colors distinguish the two groups.

## Investigation Use

Finding one witness requires navigating an institution designed to make every resident administratively interchangeable.
