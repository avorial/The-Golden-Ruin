# Léo Taxil

<div class="wiki-layout">
<div class="wiki-main">

<section class="wiki-section">
<h2>At The Table</h2>
<p>Taxil grins like a man already composing tomorrow's denial. He is funny, venomous, and happiest when a respectable person cannot decide whether to strike him or quote him.</p>
</section>

<section class="wiki-section">
<h2>History</h2>
<p>Taxil built a career on provocation, fraud, and public scandal, especially around religion and secret societies. His talent is knowing which audience wants to be outraged and which wants to be fooled.</p>
</section>

<section class="wiki-section">
<h2>Campaign Use</h2>
<p>He is a magnificent unreliable witness. A Taxil story may be false in every detail while still pointing directly at the monster he used as decoration.</p>
</section>

## Hidden Truth

Taxil invented a demon for a pamphlet and later found its name scratched into his own desk. He refuses to decide whether this proves him brilliant or doomed.

## GM Ideas

- Taxil hires the characters to disprove a cult that is imitating one of his hoaxes.
- A slanderous pamphlet contains an accidental ritual diagram.
- His public recantation becomes the cover for a private sacrifice.
:::
