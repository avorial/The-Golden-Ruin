# Marble Dancer

## Overview

By day the Marble Dancer is an androgynous statue. At night it moves with impossible grace and searches for a lover whose vitality might make it human. Each meeting ages the victim until death returns the Dancer to its pedestal.

## Investigation and Response

- Museum staff find footprints and displaced objects near the statue.
- A young lover ages dramatically over a handful of nights.
- The victim defends the Dancer and describes the affair as transcendent.
- Drawing out the statue's longing and true nature creates an opening.

Breaking the cycle may require separating it from its chosen lover, persuading it that its transformation is impossible, or destroying the sculpture before the final exchange.
