# La Santé Prison

## Overview

Its varied population and isolation cells make it useful for police interviews, hidden witnesses, and inmates who must not communicate.

## Historical Background

La Santé Prison (named after its location on the Rue de la Santé) (French: Maison d'arrêt de la Santé or Prison de la Santé) is a prison operated by the French Prison Service of the Ministry of Justice located in the east of the Montparnasse district of the 14th arrondissement in southern Paris, France, at 42 Rue de la Santé. It is one of the most famous prisons in France, with both VIP and maximum security sections. La Santé is one of the three main prisons of the Paris area, along with Fleury-Mérogis (Europe's largest prison) and Fresnes, both located in the southern suburbs.

This background is adapted from the [La Santé Prison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Sant%C3%A9_Prison) article on Wikipedia (en), accessed September 23, 2026, under the CC BY-SA license.
