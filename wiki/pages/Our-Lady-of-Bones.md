# Our Lady of Bones

## Overview

Our Lady of Bones is not an old saint but a recent Carcosan invention. She appears as a petite, consoling woman when sated and gathers mourners into a private devotion. While feeding, her true form is a mass of worms, bones, organs, and tallow.

## Investigation and Response

- No church record mentions the saint before the current season.
- Grieving worshipers receive eerily precise messages from the dead.
- Devotees disappear after private rites at [[Notre-Dame de Paris]].
- Offerings contain human tissue or objects taken from graves.

She manipulates grief and death rather than offering solace. Prayer and sacrifice may make her less immediately hostile but deepen the false cult; exposing her invented history is the safer path.
