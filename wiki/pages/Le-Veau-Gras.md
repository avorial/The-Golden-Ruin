# Le Veau Gras

## Overview

Its art-student and school-worker clientele eats simple food and drinks improbably cheap wine under the eye of Pierre, the florid, cleft-nosed proprietor. Behind the bar stands a vodka bottle containing tea leaves curled like a monkey’s paw.

## Investigation Use

Pierre says the time has not yet come to explain the bottle. A missing student, whispered clue, or altered object can first surface here.
