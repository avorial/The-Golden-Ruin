# Protestant Churches of Paris

## Overview

The British Embassy maintains an Anglican chapel; other English-speaking churches stand in Passy, on Rue Bayard, Rue Roquépine, Rue Royale, and Rue de Lille.

## Investigation Use

A frightened foreign witness chooses a familiar church, forcing investigators to identify which congregation received the confession.
