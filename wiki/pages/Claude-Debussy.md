# Claude Debussy

<div class="wiki-layout">
<div class="wiki-main">

<section class="wiki-section">
<h2>At The Table</h2>
<p>Claude Debussy has the impatience of someone who knows he is right before anyone else has found the question. He can be charming when curiosity takes him and cruel when boredom does.</p>
</section>

<section class="wiki-section">
<h2>History</h2>
<p>By 1895 Debussy has already written music that will outlive most of his admirers, though Paris has not yet fully understood what it has. He moves through Symbolist and mystical circles, carrying domestic drama, debt, and artistic certainty with him.</p>
</section>

<section class="wiki-section">
<h2>Campaign Use</h2>
<p>Debussy can connect the characters to Symbolist poets, occult salons, and music that seems to remember a place no musician has visited. He is best used as a brilliant witness, difficult ally, or beautiful bad idea.</p>
</section>
<section class="wiki-section">
<h2>Sourcebook Profile</h2>
<ul>
<li>A former child prodigy who pursues life willfully and has little patience for people he considers less brilliant.</li>
<li>Heavy brows, unruly dark hair, and immediate complaints announce his stubborn temperament.</li>
<li>He lives with Gaby Dupont, whom he treats as the practical restraint on his affairs and spending.</li>
<li>By 1895 he has composed Clair de lune and Prélude à l’après-midi d’un faune, though their lasting importance is not yet evident.</li>
<li>Wagnerian mysticism, Symbolist poets, and Rosicrucian texts provide several routes into an occult investigation.</li>
</ul>
<h3>Published Scenario Uses</h3>
<ul>
<li>A musical pattern, Symbolist friendship, or Rosicrucian score can make him a brilliant but volatile consultant.</li>
</ul>
</section>

## Hidden Truth

Debussy has heard a sequence in dreams that should not be playable on a piano. When written down, the notes resemble windows arranged around a courtyard.

## GM Ideas

- A student at [[House of the North Star]] plays Debussy's dream phrase before meeting him.
- A Rosicrucian collector wants Debussy to score a private reading of the play.
- Debussy can identify Carcosan influence by silence: the wrong pause between notes.
:::
