# Green Fairy

## Overview

Absinthe does not create this hallucination; it lets the drinker see through it. A Green Fairy passes as human until intoxicated eyes reveal rippling green skin, iron claws, predatory teeth, and the webbing of a spawning lair.

## Signs and Investigation

- Different drinkers describe the same impossible figure.
- Victims lose vitality without visible injury and become listless or dependent.
- Sticky strands and translucent egg sacs appear in locked rooms.
- The creature's apparent age and vigor rise as its victim's decline.

## Hidden Truth

Green Fairies transfer stolen energy and can keep prey alive for repeated feeding. A recently glutted creature is slower and easier to confront; one actively drawing power from an investigator is much stronger.
