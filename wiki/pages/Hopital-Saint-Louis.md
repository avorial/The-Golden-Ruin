# Hôpital Saint-Louis

## Overview

Its long institutional history and working-district setting make it a practical destination away from fashionable scrutiny.

## Historical Background

Hôpital Saint-Louis (French pronunciation: [opital sɛ̃ lwi]) is a hospital in Paris, France. It was built in 1611 by architect Claude Vellefaux at the request of Henry IV of France. It is part of the Assistance publique - Hôpitaux de Paris hospital system, and it is located at 1 avenue Claude-Vellefaux, in the 10th arrondissement near the metro station Goncourt.

This background is adapted from the [Hôpital Saint-Louis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H%C3%B4pital_Saint-Louis) article on Wikipedia (en), accessed September 23, 2026, under the CC BY-SA license.
