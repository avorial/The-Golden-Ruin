# Pigment

## Overview

A subversive paint concern imports Carcosan ingredients and sells colors engineered to induce frenzy, nightmares, or sleep. Its clients use the paints to ruin aristocrats and destabilize respectable institutions.

## Investigation and Response

- Different paintings provoke the same precise symptoms.
- Botanical analysis identifies ingredients that should not exist in France.
- Ledgers connect radical patrons, artists, and a guarded supplier.
- A concealed Yellow Sign alerts the King when the operation is threatened.

The makers have been altered by repeated exposure and can sedate through touch. Tracing the supply chain matters as much as raiding the workshop, since surviving stock can restart the scheme.
