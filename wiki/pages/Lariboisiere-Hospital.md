# Lariboisière Hospital

## Overview

Its institutional experience makes it the natural destination for advice about infectious disease and citywide outbreaks.

## Historical Background

Lariboisière Hospital (French: Hôpital Lariboisière, pronounced [opital laʁibwazjɛʁ]) is a hospital in the 10th arrondissement of Paris, France. The hospital was one of several built following the second cholera pandemic, which had reached Paris in 1832, and which led to a new emphasis on hygienic practices in hospitals. It was built from 1846 to 1853 under architect Pierre Gauthier, with six buildings arranged around a central courtyard, connected by colonnaded walkways.

This background is adapted from the [Lariboisière Hospital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lariboisi%C3%A8re_Hospital) article on Wikipedia (en), accessed September 23, 2026, under the CC BY-SA license.
