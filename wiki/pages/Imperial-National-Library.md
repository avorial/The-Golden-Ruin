# Imperial National Library

## Overview

Known before and after this period as the Bibliothèque Nationale, its vast holdings make it the obvious destination for dangerous texts.

## Investigation Use

The investigators must prevent a copy of The King in Yellow from entering the catalogue, or remove one already shelved.
