# Imperial National Library

## Overview

Known before and after this period as the Bibliothèque Nationale, its vast holdings make it the obvious destination for dangerous texts.

## Historical Background

The Richelieu complex fills the block bounded by rues de Richelieu, Vivienne, Colbert, and des Petits-Champs. Its buildings began as seventeenth-century private residences and were successively adapted by architects including François Mansart, Robert de Cotte, Henri Labrouste, and Jean-Louis Pascal for the national collections.

This background is adapted from the [Richelieu - Bibliothèques, musée, galeries](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richelieu_-_Biblioth%C3%A8ques%2C_mus%C3%A9e%2C_galeries) article on Wikipedia, accessed September 23, 2026, under the CC BY-SA license.
