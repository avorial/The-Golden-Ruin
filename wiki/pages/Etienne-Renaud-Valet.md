# Étienne Renaud, Valet

<div class="wiki-layout">
<div class="wiki-main">

<section class="wiki-section">
<h2>At The Table</h2>
<p>Étienne Renaud is elegant in the way a blade is elegant: narrow, exact, and easy to underestimate until it is already too close. He dresses Lucien de Vauclair, maintains the master's rooms, handles delicate luggage, and appears whenever a gentleman guest discovers that Paris dust, rain, or panic has spoiled his appearance.</p>

<p>He speaks softly and rarely offers an opinion. When he does, it lands with the peculiar force of a man who has been listening for a long time.</p>
</section>

<section class="wiki-section">
<h2>Place In The House</h2>
<p>Renaud belongs to the private side of the North Star. Burke commands the household; Renaud manages the surfaces closest to Lucien: collars, gloves, letters left on a dressing table, medicine bottles, half-finished sketches, and the little evidence a gentleman leaves behind when he believes no servant counts.</p>

<p>He is often sent to prepare rooms for important male guests, particularly Americans whose manners require quiet correction before dinner.</p>
</section>

<section class="wiki-section">
<h2>Public Face</h2>
<p>To visitors, Renaud is helpful, dry, and impossible to embarrass. He can repair a torn cuff, identify a perfume, summon a cab, or remove a wine stain while making the ruined garment seem at fault rather than the wearer.</p>

<p>Students find him fascinating because he seems to know adult secrets without being old. Renaud encourages this mistake only when it lets him learn what the students are hiding.</p>
</section>

## Hidden Truth

Renaud is not a full member of the Caretakers, but Burke has begun preparing him. He has been taught to recognize certain signs: repeated star sketches, cold rooms where no window is open, letters whose ink changes under candlelight, and students who wake speaking in voices not their own.

Renaud resents being kept half-informed. He believes Burke trusts him with errands but not truth, and that resentment makes him vulnerable to anyone who treats him as an equal.

## What Renaud Is Hiding

Renaud has found a set of Lucien's stained gloves wrapped in brown paper and hidden behind the false back of a wardrobe. The stains look like paint in daylight and blood under lamplight.

He has not told Burke yet.

## Pressure Moves

- Renaud offers to clean or repair an incriminating garment, then keeps one thread or button as insurance.
- Renaud admits he saw someone leave Lucien's room but refuses to name them in public.
- Renaud asks the characters whether Burke has told them about the Caretakers before realizing they have never heard the word.
:::
