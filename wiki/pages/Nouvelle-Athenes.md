# Nouvelle Athènes

## Overview

Degas holds court here and used it as the setting for L’Absinthe. Gustave Moreau may dine after teaching at the École des Beaux-Arts.

## Historical Background

The Nouvelle Athènes, or Café de la Nouvelle-Athènes, was a café located at 66 Rue Pigalle in the Place Pigalle in the quartier Nouvelle Athènes, Paris, France. It was the setting for many Impressionist paintings, as a result of being the meeting place for painters, including Henri Matisse, Vincent van Gogh and Edgar Degas.

This background is adapted from the [La Nouvelle Athènes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Nouvelle_Ath%C3%A8nes) article on Wikipedia (en), accessed September 23, 2026, under the CC BY-SA license.
