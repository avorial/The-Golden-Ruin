# Notre-Dame de Paris

## Overview

Begun in 1163 and repeatedly damaged, altered, and restored, it holds gargoyles, long-beaked chimeras, and relics identified as the Crown of Thorns, part of the True Cross, and a Holy Nail.

## Investigation Use

Stone grotesques begin changing position while a relic produces the wrong miracle.
