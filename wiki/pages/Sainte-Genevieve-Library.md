# Sainte-Geneviève Library

## Overview

Opened in 1843 around a monastic collection begun in the twelfth century, it now tries to recover important works sold away during the seventeenth century.

## Investigation Use

A lost monastic book returns bearing fresh annotations in an impossible hand.
