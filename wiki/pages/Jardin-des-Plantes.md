# Jardin des Plantes

## Overview

The Ménagerie du Jardin des Plantes has been rebuilt after starving Parisians ate much of its animal collection during the Siege.

## Historical Background

The Jardin des Plantes (French pronunciation: [ʒaʁdɛ̃ de plɑ̃t] , lit. 'Garden of the Plants'), known as the Jardin des Plantes de Paris (French: [- də paʁi]) when distinguished from those in other cities, and formerly as the Jardin du Roi, is the main botanical garden in France. Jardin des Plantes is the official name, shortened from Jardin Royal des Plantes Médicinales, as the original purpose of the garden in the 17th century was to grow medicinal plants.
