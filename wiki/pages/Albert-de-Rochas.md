# Albert de Rochas

<div class="wiki-layout">
<div class="wiki-main">

<section class="wiki-section">
<h2>At The Table</h2>
<p>De Rochas is exacting, dry, and quietly fearless. He treats trance work as a field exercise: instruments prepared, witnesses placed, escape routes noted.</p>
</section>

<section class="wiki-section">
<h2>History</h2>
<p>Trained in military and technical circles, de Rochas studies hypnotism, magnetism, and strange claims of memory across lives. His curiosity is scholarly, but his habits remain those of a soldier.</p>
</section>

<section class="wiki-section">
<h2>Campaign Use</h2>
<p>He is a strong contact for experiments, occult technology, and disciplined investigation. When other occultists embroider, de Rochas asks what happens if the phenomenon is repeated under pressure.</p>
</section>
