# Café Guerbois

## Overview

Cézanne, Degas, Renoir, Zola, and their peers are older and visit less often, but nostalgia or the right invitation can draw them back to their former Thursday and Sunday table.

## Investigation Use

A reunion places several famous witnesses around one contradictory memory of the same painting.
