# Saint-Sulpice

## Overview

The current building began in 1646. Aristide Cavaillé-Coll rebuilt its organ in 1862, and composer Charles-Marie Widor holds the prestigious organist’s post.

## Historical Background

The Church of Saint-Sulpice (French pronunciation: [sɛ̃ sylpis] ) is a Catholic church in Paris, France, on the east side of Place Saint-Sulpice, in the 6th arrondissement. It is the second largest church in the city. It is dedicated to Sulpitius the Pious.

This background is adapted from the [Saint-Sulpice, Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint-Sulpice,_Paris) article on Wikipedia (en), accessed September 23, 2026, under the CC BY-SA license.

## Investigation Use

A musical clue requires access to Widor, the organ loft, or a tone that the instrument should be unable to produce.
