# Pan

> Content warning: this entry involves stalking, coercion, and sexual violence.

## Overview

Carcosan influence has made Pan and Syrinx real. Pan hunts Syrinx while deceiving outsiders into helping him. Syrinx's living body has been transformed into flutes; her song prevents Pan from perceiving where she is.

## Investigation and Response

- Pan presents himself as a wronged lover seeking a kidnapped woman.
- Accounts of his story subtly contradict one another.
- An impossible flute melody disrupts his attention and physical presence.
- Only Syrinx can directly harm Pan in his incorporeal state; otherwise he must possess a body to act.

Restoring Syrinx and allowing her to confront him is the surest end. Killing Pan frees her from the transformation.
