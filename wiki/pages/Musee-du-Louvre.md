# Musée du Louvre

## Overview

Its collections include the Venus de Milo, Mona Lisa, and the chapel from the tomb of Akhethetep. Living artists cannot yet expect their work to hang here.

## Historical Background

The Louvre (French: Musée du Louvre [myze dy luvʁ] ) is a national art museum in Paris, France. It is located on the Right Bank of the Seine in the city's 1st arrondissement (district) and home to some of the most canonical works of Western art, including the Mona Lisa, Venus de Milo, and Winged Victory. The museum is housed in the Louvre Palace, originally built in the late 12th to 13th century under Philip II.
