# Musée du Louvre

## Overview

Its collections include the Venus de Milo, Mona Lisa, and the chapel from the tomb of Akhethetep. Living artists cannot yet expect their work to hang here.

## Investigation Use

A dead artist’s newly accepted work changes after installation, or an antiquity no longer matches its catalogue image.
