# Basilica of Saint-Denis

## Overview

The basilica’s burials reach back to Roman times and include nearly every French king from the tenth through eighteenth centuries. Its present core helped establish Gothic architecture.

## Investigation Use

A royal tomb contains the wrong bones, or a monarch’s remains have acquired a second shadow.
