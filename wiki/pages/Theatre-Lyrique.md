# Théâtre Lyrique

## Overview

The Haussmann-era building burned during the Commune and was rebuilt to the same design in 1874. Spoken dialogue defines opéra-comique, not humor; Sarah Bernhardt will later take over the venue.

## Investigation Use

A performance with spoken passages conceals a ritual that the sung score alone does not reveal.
