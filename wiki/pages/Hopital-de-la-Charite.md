# Hôpital de la Charité

## Overview

Founded at Marie de’ Medici’s request, it joins indigent care with medical authority in the academy’s converted chapel offices.

## Historical Background

Hôpital de la Charité (French pronunciation: [opital də la ʃaʁite], "Charity Hospital") was a hospital in Paris founded by the Brothers Hospitallers of St. John of God in the 17th century. In 1935, it was closed and demolished to make way for the new faculty of medicine.

This background is adapted from the [Hôpital de la Charité](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H%C3%B4pital_de_la_Charit%C3%A9) article on Wikipedia (en), accessed September 23, 2026, under the CC BY-SA license.

Investigators can seek expert advice while a patient’s symptoms challenge the academy’s public certainty.
