# Inkblot

## Overview

Inkblots are threadlike parasites concealed in ink, paint, and other artistic liquids. They enter through the skin, migrate toward the skull, and induce feverish bursts of creation. The resulting work and physical contact spread fresh larvae.

## Investigation and Response

- Infected artists work without sleep and produce repetitions of the Yellow Sign.
- Dark threads move under the skin toward the neck and temples.
- Shared brushes, bottles, prints, and touched artworks create a chain of cases.
- A Difficulty 5 First Aid test can remove the parasite; contaminated works and supplies must be burned.
