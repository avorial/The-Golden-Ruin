# Marie Rowdon

## Overview

Marie Rowdon is a successful painter and hostess of elite private parties fueled by absinthe and opium. When her guests are most altered, she recites fragments of *The King in Yellow*, planting the play in minds unprepared to resist it.

## Investigation and Response

- Society guests quote identical lines they cannot remember learning.
- Their personalities erode as dreams of Carcosa become more vivid.
- Invitations, servants, and suppliers connect apparently unrelated cases to Rowdon's salon.
- Rowdon produces black tears and exerts a corrupting touch when threatened.

Saving her guests requires separating them from the salon's intoxicants and interrupting the repeated recitations before the implanted text displaces their identities.
