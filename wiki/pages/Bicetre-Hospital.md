# Bicêtre Hospital

## Overview

The hospital offers another destination for those whose minds have been permanently damaged by an investigation.

## Historical Background

The Bicêtre Hospital is located in Le Kremlin-Bicêtre, a commune in the southern suburbs of Paris, France. It lies 4.5 km (2.8 miles) from the center of Paris. The Bicêtre Hospital was originally planned as a military hospital, with construction begun in 1634.

This background is adapted from the [Bicêtre Hospital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bic%C3%AAtre_Hospital) article on Wikipedia (en), accessed September 23, 2026, under the CC BY-SA license.
