# Georges Grison

<div class="wiki-layout">
<div class="wiki-main">

<section class="wiki-section">
<h2>At The Table</h2>
<p>Grison is all ink-stained cuffs and bright, hungry attention. He talks like a friend, listens like a prosecutor, and files the best line before anyone else reaches the door.</p>
</section>

<section class="wiki-section">
<h2>History</h2>
<p>A journalist drawn to crime and sensation, Grison knows the police, morgue attendants, printers, editors, and criminals who like seeing themselves in print. He understands the city's appetite for fear.</p>
</section>

<section class="wiki-section">
<h2>Campaign Use</h2>
<p>He can tip the characters to a case, complicate an investigation by publishing too soon, or preserve a clue the authorities want buried.</p>
</section>

## Hidden Truth

Grison has noticed a rise in crimes with theatrical staging. He thinks a fashionable murderer is imitating decadent literature, and he is only partly wrong.

## GM Ideas

- His latest column includes a clue he does not understand.
- A killer sends Grison scene descriptions before the crimes occur.
- Grison blackmails a cult patron and immediately regrets success.
:::
