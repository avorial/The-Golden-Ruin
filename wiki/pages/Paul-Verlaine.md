# Paul Verlaine

<div class="wiki-layout">
<div class="wiki-main">

<section class="wiki-section">
<h2>At The Table</h2>
<p>Paul Verlaine looks like a man aged by weather no one else stood under. He is revered, drunk, ruined, funny, bitter, tender, and capable of saying one line that makes the whole café fall quiet.</p>
</section>

<section class="wiki-section">
<h2>History</h2>
<p>Verlaine's life has passed through poetry, scandal, the Commune, love, violence, poverty, absinthe, and illness. By 1895 he appears in cafés and clubs where the demimonde and literati greet him with reverence despite, or because of, his collapse.</p>
</section>

<section class="wiki-section">
<h2>Campaign Use</h2>
<p>Verlaine can provide poetic prophecy, unreliable memory, or access to the lowest and highest literary circles at once. His companion Bibi-la-Purée can become a clue-bearer, nuisance, or tragic witness.</p>
</section>

## Hidden Truth

Verlaine has dreamed of a poem after his own death, attributed to him but written in a hand he does not know. Its final line is a direction to Carcosa.

## GM Ideas

- Bibi-la-Purée steals something from the characters because someone paid him in absinthe.
- Verlaine's last months become a clock for a doomed literary clue.
- A café audience finishes a verse in unison though only Verlaine knows the poem.
:::
