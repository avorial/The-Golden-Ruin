# The King in Yellow RPG Opening Situation

> A first playable scene tying the sample pages together.

## Start Here

The party arrives at [[The Thespian Theatre]] because [[Gideon Marsh]] sent a warning, a job offer, a confession, or a plea for help. [[The Audience of the Sign]] is already moving, the [[The Yellow Manuscript]] has become dangerous, and [[The Second Act]] is still deniable for one more scene.

## Immediate Questions

- Why is [[Cora Ellison]] the person who cannot ignore this?
- What does [[Gideon Marsh]] refuse to say in public?
- What does [[The Audience of the Sign]] offer that is genuinely useful?
- What sign of [[The Second Act]] proves this is more than local trouble?

## First Three Scenes

1. **Public pressure:** something obvious happens at [[The Thespian Theatre]] while witnesses are present.
2. **Private ask:** [[Gideon Marsh]] explains just enough to make refusal costly.
3. **Complication:** [[The Audience of the Sign]] arrives, claims authority, or reveals they are already one step ahead.

## Outcomes To Track

- Who leaves with the [[The Yellow Manuscript]]?
- Who learns the first real truth about [[The Second Act]]?
- Which relationship is strained before the next session?
- What page should be created next: a map, a quest, a rival, or a secret history?
