# Dark Dweller

## Overview

Someone lost beneath Paris can become a Dark Dweller: hairless, feral, uncannily strong, and unable to endure daylight. The change is gradual enough that a missing person may still be rescued before the darkness finishes remaking them.

## Signs and Investigation

- Supplies disappear from sealed underground work sites.
- A survivor recognizes a Dweller's voice, gesture, collar, or old wound.
- The creature recoils from sudden illumination and hunts confidently in total dark.
- Records of disappearances reveal a former identity and human connections.

## Hidden Truth

Darkness is not merely the creature's habitat but its sustenance. Reminding it of its past can create a hesitation; broad daylight weakens it decisively. A rescue story becomes a hunt if the investigators wait too long.
