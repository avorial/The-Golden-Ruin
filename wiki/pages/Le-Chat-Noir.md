# Le Chat Noir

## Overview

Artists and occult-minded poets gather for performances and conversation, and its house literary journal offers a route into print. Satie, Toulouse-Lautrec, and Symbolist writers may all be found here.

## Investigation Use

The venue and Salis have only a year left; a shadow-puppet performance may predict why.
