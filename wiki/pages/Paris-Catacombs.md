# Paris Catacombs

## Overview

Bones have been stacked by type since 1810, with ornamental walls of skulls and long bones. Commune dead lie near an underground stream; legal visits occur only every other Saturday.

## Historical Background

The Catacombs of Paris (French: Catacombes de Paris, ) are underground ossuaries in Paris, France, which hold the remains of more than six million people. Built to consolidate Paris's ancient stone quarries, they extend south from the Barrière d'Enfer ("Gate of Hell") former city gate. The ossuary was created as part of the effort to eliminate the effects of the city's overflowing cemeteries.

This background is adapted from the [Catacombs of Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catacombs_of_Paris) article on Wikipedia (en), accessed September 23, 2026, under the CC BY-SA license.

## Investigation Use

Newly excavated evidence is transferred here, forcing investigators to secure permission or enter by stealth before the bones disappear into the stacks.
