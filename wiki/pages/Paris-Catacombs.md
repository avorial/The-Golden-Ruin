# Paris Catacombs

## Overview

Bones have been stacked by type since 1810, with ornamental walls of skulls and long bones. Commune dead lie near an underground stream; legal visits occur only every other Saturday.

## Investigation Use

Newly excavated evidence is transferred here, forcing investigators to secure permission or enter by stealth before the bones disappear into the stacks.
