# Val-de-Grâce

## Overview

Built in the seventeenth century, it treats soldiers who often fear its care will be inferior to an expensive private hospital.

## Historical Background

The Val-de-Grâce (French pronunciation: [val də ɡʁɑs]; Hôpital d'instruction des armées du Val-de-Grâce or HIA Val-de-Grâce) was a military hospital located at 74 boulevard de Port-Royal in the 5th arrondissement of Paris, France. It was closed as a hospital in 2016.
