# Maison Fournaise

## Overview

Renoir favors its airy rooms and terraces, a marked contrast to the smoky, claustrophobic cafés of Montmartre.

## Historical Background

Maison Fournaise (French: [mɛzɔ̃ fuʁnɛz]; lit. 'Fournaise House') is a restaurant and museum located on the Île des Impressionnistes, a long island in Seine river in Chatou, west of Paris. It is best known for having been a painting place of Pierre-Auguste Renoir.

This background is adapted from the [Maison Fournaise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maison_Fournaise) article on Wikipedia (en), accessed September 23, 2026, under the CC BY-SA license.
