# Maison Fournaise

## Overview

Renoir favors its airy rooms and terraces, a marked contrast to the smoky, claustrophobic cafés of Montmartre.

## Investigation Use

A pleasant excursion becomes isolating when the river carries in an object that should have remained in Paris.
