# The Doombringers

## Overview

Thirteen gendarmes form a clandestine faction devoted to the King in Yellow. They erase evidence, redirect witnesses, protect Carcosan agents, and exploit the authority of the uniform to isolate investigators.

## Signs and Investigation

- Reports vanish or return with facts subtly reversed.
- The same officers repeatedly arrive first at supernatural incidents.
- Witnesses are intimidated into silence and then disappear.
- Members meet in the basement of an otherwise ordinary public house.

## Hidden Truth

Their shared apocalypse does not produce shared loyalty. Rank, glory, and the belief that only a favored few will be rewarded can be used to split them. Their greatest weapon is public trust in the police; their weakness is rivalry.
