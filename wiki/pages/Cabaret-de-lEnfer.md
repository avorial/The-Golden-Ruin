# Cabaret de l’Enfer

## Overview

A few doors from the Cabaret du Néant, this venue seats revelers around a cauldron while a devil-costumed band plays and impish servers carry drinks.

## Historical Background

Antonin Alexander founded this Montmartre cabaret in November 1892. Paired with the Cabaret du Ciel, it relied on elaborate atmosphere and illusion more than singers: patrons entered through the jaws of Leviathan, met costumed devils, and watched mirror-and-light effects accompanied by organ music. It moved from 34 to 53 Boulevard de Clichy in 1895.

This background is adapted from the [Cabaret de L'Enfer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabaret_de_L%27Enfer) article on Wikipedia, accessed September 23, 2026, under the CC BY-SA license.

## Investigation Use

A genuine entity can hide among the performers, relying on every witness to dismiss it as part of the show.
