# Widowmaker

## Overview

Sebastian Beaumont is a handsome aristocrat celebrated as a tragic romantic. His lovers die, evidence disappears, and Beaumont then offers every appearance of sincere cooperation to the Sûreté. He does not consciously know that he commits the murders.

## Investigation and Response

- Each victim's relationship with Beaumont was concealed for social reasons.
- His memory contains credible gaps rather than practiced lies.
- Evidence is hidden in places he can access but does not consciously recognize.
- His fame encourages police and society to protect him.

Confronting him requires proving both physical action and divided awareness. Abuse survivors are less susceptible to his cultivated vulnerability and romantic mystique.
