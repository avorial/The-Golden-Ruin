# Auguste Rodin

<div class="wiki-layout">
<div class="wiki-main">

<section class="wiki-section">
<h2>At The Table</h2>
<p>Rodin has the force of a quarrel carved into a man. He is physical, emotional, proud, and quick to anger when questions brush old accusations or suggest he cannot tell flesh from sculpture.</p>
</section>

<section class="wiki-section">
<h2>History</h2>
<p>Rodin's energetic, roughly rendered sculpture has transformed the medium and attracted fury in equal measure. Years ago critics accused him of casting <em>The Bronze Age</em> from a corpse, a charge that still stings because it misunderstood his art so completely.</p>
</section>

<section class="wiki-section">
<h2>Campaign Use</h2>
<p>Rodin can identify impossible anatomy, forged sculpture, and the difference between life captured in art and art pretending to be alive. He is not subtle, but he is hard to intimidate.</p>
</section>

| --- | --- |
| ask about living sculpture | reacts badly, then asks what they saw. |

:::gm
## Hidden Truth

Rodin has seen one sculpture that was not cast from a corpse but wanted viewers to think of death anyway. It was warm to the touch.

## GM Ideas

- Rodin identifies a statue as having changed pose after hardening.
- He knows which collector bought a grotesque figure from an occult estate sale.
- A Carcosan servant tries to commission a monument to a king no one admits exists.
:::
