# Alexandre Saint-Yves

<div class="wiki-layout">
<div class="wiki-main">

<section class="wiki-section">
<h2>At The Table</h2>
<p>Saint-Yves speaks as though history is an instrument currently out of tune. He is courteous, paternal, and certain that ordinary politics is merely the shadow of a better invisible order.</p>
</section>

<section class="wiki-section">
<h2>History</h2>
<p>Saint-Yves writes of mystical governance, sacred hierarchy, and hidden centers of wisdom. He attracts readers who want politics, revelation, and cosmic architecture to be the same subject.</p>
</section>

<section class="wiki-section">
<h2>Campaign Use</h2>
<p>He can connect occultism to diplomacy, conspiracies, utopian schemes, and aristocratic circles. His ideas make excellent bait for cults that want a divine mandate.</p>
</section>

## Hidden Truth

Saint-Yves has received letters from a supposed hidden city. The handwriting changes, but the yellow ink never does.

## GM Ideas

- His correspondents invite the characters to a lecture that becomes a recruitment test.
- A murder scene is staged according to one of his diagrams.
- He recognizes a Carcosan symbol as a parody of his own ideal order.
:::
