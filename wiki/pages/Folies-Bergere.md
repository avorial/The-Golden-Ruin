# Folies Bergère

## Overview

Performers such as Loie Fuller bring dance, costume, and experimental light to its stage. Its 1895 spectacle is provocative without the nudity associated with the venue in later decades.

## Investigation Use

An impossible lighting effect or missing dancer can turn a revue into an investigation.
