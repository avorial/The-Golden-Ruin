# Madame Cleo Verlaque

## Overview

Madame Verlaque cultivates grieving, wealthy clients through private séances. Yellow flowers, pungent herbs, a black metallic bowl, and lines from *The King in Yellow* allow extra-dimensional Carcosan entities to enter through her body.

## Investigation and Response

- Clients report accurate messages followed by invasive shared nightmares.
- The same yellow flowers and metallic residue appear after every sitting.
- Servants conceal a private ritual room in Verlaque's home.
- During possession, her voice and posture change faster than ordinary performance permits.

The summoned minds inflict psychic horrors rather than simple fraud. Interrupting the ritual, removing its anchor objects, and protecting the bereaved from renewed contact can close the route.
