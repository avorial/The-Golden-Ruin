# Tour Saint-Jacques

## Overview

After the Commune, the surrounding park served as a hurried mass grave, with reports of shallow burials and people interred alive. Nicolas Flamel was once buried beneath the vanished church.

## Investigation Use

The dead stir around the tower as investigators search for Flamel’s displaced remains.
