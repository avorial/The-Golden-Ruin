# Camille Flammarion

<div class="wiki-layout">
<div class="wiki-main">

<section class="wiki-section">
<h2>At The Table</h2>
<p>Camille Flammarion has the grand air of a man who expects the universe to be interesting and is rarely disappointed. He moves easily between astronomy, spiritism, fiction, and lectures, drawing maps of worlds no one else has visited.</p>
</section>

<section class="wiki-section">
<h2>History</h2>
<p>A polymath astronomer and popularizer, Flammarion believes in evolution, transmigration of souls, life on other worlds, and scientific inquiry into séances. He appears wherever science and occult curiosity overlap.</p>
</section>

<section class="wiki-section">
<h2>Campaign Use</h2>
<p>Flammarion can recruit investigators through scientific and occult networks. He is helpful, persuasive, and often wrong in a productive way.</p>
</section>

## Hidden Truth

Flammarion's theories about souls migrating to other worlds can accidentally describe Carcosan displacement. He is close enough to be useful and wrong enough to be dangerous.

## GM Ideas

- Flammarion sends the characters after book copies through astronomical society contacts.
- A star chart in his possession includes Aldebaran where no chart should.
- He mistakes a Carcosan messenger for evidence of extraterrestrial soul travel.
:::
