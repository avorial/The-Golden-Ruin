# Temple Square

## Overview

The Temple once imprisoned the royal family and attracted Royalist pilgrims until Napoléon ordered its destruction in 1808. Allegorical statues represent Hope, Justice, Abundance, and Prudence.

## Historical Background

Created in 1857, the Square du Temple was one of the landscaped squares planned under Haussmann and Alphand. It occupies part of the former Enclos du Temple, the fortified Paris precinct established by the Knights Templar and later used as a royal prison.
