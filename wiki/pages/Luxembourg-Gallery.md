# Luxembourg Gallery

## Overview

The gallery displays current work by figures such as Rodin and Monet and appears in Chambers’s “The Mask.” It has occupied this building since 1884.

## Investigation Use

A sculpture or painting changes material overnight while guards insist nothing moved.
