# Sainte-Chapelle

## Overview

Once part of the medieval royal palace, the chapel received an acclaimed restoration forty years ago and remains one of the island’s most concentrated displays of sacred kingship.

## Investigation Use

A pane of glass introduces a crowned figure absent from every restoration record.
