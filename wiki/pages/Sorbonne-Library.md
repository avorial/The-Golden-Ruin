# Sorbonne Library

## Overview

The university is about to resume its traditional name, the Sorbonne. Its academic collections and scholars support broad historical and theoretical research.

## Historical Background

The Sorbonne Library (French: Bibliothèque interuniversitaire de la Sorbonne) is an inter-university library part of the network of 36 libraries of the Panthéon-Sorbonne University, in Paris, France. It is located at 47, rue des Écoles in the Latin Quarter in the 5th arrondissement. The library of the Institute of Geography, located at 191 rue Saint-Jacques, is attached.

This background is adapted from the [Sorbonne Library](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sorbonne_Library) article on Wikipedia (en), accessed September 23, 2026, under the CC BY-SA license.
