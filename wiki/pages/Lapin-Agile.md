# Lapin Agile

## Overview

Older patrons still call it the Cabaret of Assassins. Its newer name comes from the sign of a rabbit escaping a frying pan; the artists have not yet fully displaced the underworld clientele.

## Historical Background

Lapin Agile [la.pɛ̃ a.ʒil] is a famous Montmartre cabaret, at 22 Rue des Saules, 18th arrondissement of Paris, France.
