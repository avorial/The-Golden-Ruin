# La Madeleine

## Overview

Napoléon ordered the structure as a monument to his army before the Restoration redirected it toward its earlier intended use as a church. Its architecture retains both identities.

## Historical Background

The Madeleine is a monumental neoclassical church in the 8th arrondissement. Political upheaval repeatedly changed both its plans and purpose: Napoleon recast it in 1806 as a temple to the glory of the Grande Armée, it was considered as a railway station in 1837, and it finally became a church in 1845.

This background is adapted from the [Église de la Madeleine](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%89glise_de_la_Madeleine) article on Wikipedia, accessed September 23, 2026, under the CC BY-SA license.

## Investigation Use
