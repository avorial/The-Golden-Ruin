# Hôtel de Ville

## Overview

The restored building combines surviving sixteenth-century stone with an opulent modern interior. Investigators come here for permits, bureaucrats, and municipal records.

## Historical Background

Paris's city hall stands in the 4th arrondissement. The older building was burned during the final days of the Commune in May 1871, destroying the municipal archives within; its exterior was reconstructed on an enlarged version of the old design between 1874 and 1882, while the interior was substantially redesigned.
