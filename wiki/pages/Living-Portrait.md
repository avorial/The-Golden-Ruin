# Living Portrait

## Overview

Painter Clement Medin mixes a yellowish Carcosan solvent into his work. Finished portraits breathe, change expression, and conceal skull motifs. Each is a doorway into a pocket dimension capable of swallowing salon guests.

## Investigation and Response

- A subject's painted eyes follow people across the room.
- Missing guests were last seen standing before the same canvas.
- Turpentine with an unusual yellow cast appears in Medin's studio.
- The Yellow Sign is hidden in underpainting or decorative detail.

Destroying the visible canvas is risky while victims remain inside. The investigators must enter or destabilize the pocket world, recover survivors, and end the painting's connection to the solvent.
