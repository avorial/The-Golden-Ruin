# Silver Sirens

## Overview

Silver Sirens are fish by day, women by night, and disturbing hybrids at twilight. Their psychic sonar identifies men who have committed violence against women or fish. They lure those targets from the banks of the Seine, kill them, and feed.

## Investigation and Response

- Bodies recovered from the river share a history of concealed abuse.
- Women and fishermen tell incompatible stories about pale singers on the quay.
- Half-human tracks appear only around dawn and dusk.
- The Sirens will not harm women, fish, or another Siren.

Their certainty about guilt can be manipulated, and their instinctive trust of women is exploitable. Investigators must decide whether stopping the killings also means exposing the victims' crimes.
