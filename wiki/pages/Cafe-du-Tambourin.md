# Café du Tambourin

## Overview

Gauguin may visit before leaving permanently for Tahiti in June 1895, remembering the café as a place associated with happier days.

## Historical Background

Café du Tambourin was a restaurant in Paris, France. Owned by Agostina Segatori, it was opened in December 1883 at 27 rue de Richelieu, and then in March 1885 relocated at 62 Boulevard de Clichy. Famous painter, Jules Chéret, made a poster for the Cabaret at the reopening.

This background is adapted from the [Café du Tambourin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caf%C3%A9_du_Tambourin) article on Wikipedia (en), accessed September 23, 2026, under the CC BY-SA license.
