# Liberty Vintners

## Overview

After blight ruined their ordinary vines, six vintners grafted in ruby cuttings from Carcosa. Their Vin de la Liberté removes inhibition and restraint, making fashionable drinkers easy to compromise. Daily use also halts aging.

## Investigation and Response

- Important people behave recklessly after private tastings.
- Bottles have falsified origins and pass through guarded distributors.
- Longtime customers look decades younger than their records suggest.
- Botanical or winemaking expertise exposes the impossible grafts.

The vintners are human and protect their secret with bribery, blackmail, and armed force. Destroying the vines ends production but may also provoke every powerful customer dependent on the wine.
