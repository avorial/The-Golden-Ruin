# Stanislas de Guaita

<div class="wiki-layout">
<div class="wiki-main">

<section class="wiki-section">
<h2>At The Table</h2>
<p>Stanislas de Guaita has the bearing of an aristocrat who believes evil is real and that he has been properly educated to fight it. He is earnest, romantic, and so committed to ritual categories that he may misname the danger while opposing it bravely.</p>
</section>

<section class="wiki-section">
<h2>History</h2>
<p>De Guaita co-founded the Ordre Kabbalistique de la Rose-Croix with Joséphin Péladan before their schism. He investigated Abbé Boullan and became embroiled in accusations of magical warfare with Huysmans and rival occultists.</p>
</section>

<section class="wiki-section">
<h2>Campaign Use</h2>
<p>De Guaita is a strong patron for occult combat. He can direct the characters against the King while understanding Carcosa as black magic, a misreading that may cause as many problems as it solves.</p>
</section>

## Hidden Truth

De Guaita's battle against black magic is sincere, but his framework cannot contain Carcosa. If the PCs rely on him too completely, they may fight the wrong war beautifully.

## GM Ideas

- De Guaita tasks the characters with retrieving a grimoire before Péladan's order gets it.
- His unfinished manuscript contains one passage he did not write.
- He can provide ritual protection that works once, then attracts attention.
:::
