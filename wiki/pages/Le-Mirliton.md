# Le Mirliton

## Overview

Bruant performs for wealthy Parisians and foreign visitors who come to be abused. Chamber pots and a Louis XIII chair suspended from the ceiling announce the venue’s cultivated irreverence.

## Historical Background

Aristide Bruant opened Le Mirliton in July 1885 at 84 Boulevard Rochechouart, in the former rooms of Le Chat Noir. Its mixed clientele made the place fashionable, while Bruant's rough wordplay and songs drew on street life. Toulouse-Lautrec became a regular between 1888 and 1892, exhibited work there, and designed a poster for its weekly paper.
