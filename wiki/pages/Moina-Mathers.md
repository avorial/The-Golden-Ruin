# Moina Mathers

<div class="wiki-layout">
<div class="wiki-main">

<section class="wiki-section">
<h2>At The Table</h2>
<p>Moina Mathers moves like a Pre-Raphaelite painting that learned to speak French and conduct ritual. She is poised, artistic, feminist in bearing if not always in doctrine, and difficult to reduce to anyone's assistant.</p>
</section>

<section class="wiki-section">
<h2>History</h2>
<p>Moina and Samuel Liddell "MacGregor" Mathers relocated to Paris under the direction of the Secret Chiefs, at least according to Samuel. Moina spent part of her childhood in Paris, speaks the language well, creates art, and serves as a co-ritualist in the Golden Dawn project.</p>
</section>

<section class="wiki-section">
<h2>Campaign Use</h2>
<p>Moina is the Mathers most likely to command a room. She can introduce English occult visitors, ritual art, and the couple's coming Isis-themed performances.</p>
</section>

## Hidden Truth

Moina's ritual art may be safer than Samuel's ceremonial theory. Her drawings sometimes show what a rite will summon before the rite is performed.

## GM Ideas

- Moina sketches a Carcosan figure while designing Isis robes.
- She privately asks the characters to verify one of Samuel's messages from the Secret Chiefs.
- Her childhood memories of Paris include a North Star door that should not have existed.
:::
