# The Dollmaker

## Overview

Evelyn Royer's shop sells exquisite dolls with disquietingly familiar faces. In the basement she uses a bodily token from each victim and a Carcosan altar to bind a soul into its likeness, leaving the original body alive but vacant.

## Signs and Investigation

- A doll resembles a missing person down to a private scar or garment.
- Victims remain breathing but cannot be roused.
- Hair, nail clippings, blood, or stolen keepsakes vanish shortly before each attack.
- The basement contains an altar and space prepared for three final dolls.

## Hidden Truth

Royer needs three more souls to open a rift to Carcosa. Breaking a completed doll releases its captive, but careless destruction amid the ritual may accelerate the breach. Royer is human, frightened of dolls herself, and more dangerous when surrounded by her collection.
