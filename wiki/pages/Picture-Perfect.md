# Picture Perfect

## Overview

Admiration for a dead artistic giant condenses into a personalized shade. At first only devoted artists can perceive it. As it recruits new followers, it gains substance, strength, and the ability to impose an alien ideal of beauty.

## Investigation and Response

- Artists independently report private instruction from the same dead master.
- Their new work grows technically brilliant and personally empty.
- Only people with a meaningful connection to the relevant art can see the shade at first.
- Each converted follower makes it harder to oppose.

The investigators can deny it its next victim, expose the contradictions in its teachings, and disrupt collective belief before it becomes fully corporeal.
