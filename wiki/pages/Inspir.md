# Inspir

## Overview

An Inspir attaches itself to as many as four artists. Its presence first feels like miraculous inspiration. Dream-whispers then foster obsession, dependence, and violence until the artists care only for pleasing their muse.

## Investigation and Response

- Several artists describe the same elusive lover or patron.
- Their strongest new works share imagery none can explain.
- Victims become hostile when separated from their work or questioned about the muse.
- Killing the physical Inspir releases its living victims. An artist who previously escaped and made art about the ordeal has an advantage against it.
