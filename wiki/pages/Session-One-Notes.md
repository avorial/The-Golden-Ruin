## Arrival at the House of the North Star

The characters arrived at the **[[House of the North Star]]**, where **[[Silas Burke, Head Butler|Silas Burke]]** welcomed them. Burke explained the house rules, introduced the staff, and allowed everyone to choose their rooms.

### [[Marcus-J-rn-Jensen-Yoshi|Marcus “Járn” Jensen]]

Marcus discovered a small collection of **gears made from a strange, unfamiliar metal** while wandering through the house. He kept them.

### [[Edward-Vine-Jarod|Edward Vine]]

Edward found a strange **pig pen** while exploring the grounds. One pig stood and stared at him in an unnerving manner. Inside its feeding trough he found a **bloodstained lumberjack shirt**.

Edward secretly took the shirt back to the house, cleaned it, and hid it among his belongings.

### [[Jackson-Jay-Steerman-IV-David|Jackson “Jay” Steerman IV]]

Jackson began studying occult books available at the North Star. He became particularly interested in **wards and protective symbols**, including wards used to exclude or restrain the undead. This became important during the gathering at Bonnet's house.

## House Staff

- **[[Silas Burke, Head Butler|Silas Burke]], Head Butler and House Manager:** Formal, controlled, and extremely observant. Burke seems to know nearly everything entering or leaving the North Star.
- **[[Élise Marceau, Maid|Élise Marceau]], Maid:** Friendly and observant, with a talent for noticing emotional details and interpersonal tensions.
- **[[Madame Eugénie Voclain, Head Cook|Madame Eugénie Voclain]], Cook and Housekeeper:** Knew Nell Whitcomb during Nell's time at the North Star and remembers her fondly.
- **[[Étienne Renaud, Valet|Étienne Renaud]], Valet and Footman:** Handles luggage, messages, errands, visitors, and dealings with outside servants.

## The Sculpture Unveiling

The group attended a private sculpture unveiling at [[Hôtel Bonnet]], home of **[[Dr. Georges Bonnet]]**. The centerpiece was Bonnet's sculpture of **[[Eleanor “Nell” Whitcomb]]**, a former North Star resident.

### [[Dr. Georges Bonnet]]

Bonnet hosted the evening and presented his new sculpture of Nell.

While moving a piece of furniture, Jackson accidentally disturbed or broke what appeared to be an occult **ward**. Immediately afterward, Bonnet's hand briefly turned an unnatural **yellow color**. He concealed it and continued as though nothing had happened.

The incident suggested that the ward might have been preserving Bonnet or keeping something dead alive.

Bonnet had been romantically involved with Nell. He also had an earlier relationship with [[Janine Du Bois]] while they were in Venice. Although that relationship ended, emotional tension remains between them. Bonnet possesses a **hidden sculpture of Janine** not included in the public exhibition.

Bonnet also knows [[Dr. Ludwig Weiss]] from many years earlier. Their conversation suggested that Weiss might once have been Bonnet's medical teacher or mentor.

### [[Eleanor “Nell” Whitcomb]]

Nell, a twenty-four-year-old American artist, dancer, and former North Star resident, was the model for Bonnet's sculpture. She recognized the name of **Edward's patron** and apparently knew him.

Nell invited the group to meet her later at a restaurant near the North Star. She gave Edward a small drawing made on the back of a calling card belonging to **[[Louis Lépine]]**, Prefect of Police.

She also spoke about **Roberts**, another former North Star resident and sculptor. Roberts is currently in **Florence**, and his family made its fortune in the **Canadian lumber business**.

At the end of the session, **[[Louis Lépine]] arrived and announced that Nell Whitcomb was dead.**

### [[Dr. Ludwig Weiss]]

Weiss is an elderly Austrian physician famous for experimental surgery. He said that he had known Bonnet for many years and that they had worked together before. Their history appeared old enough that Weiss might once have served as Bonnet's medical teacher or mentor.

Weiss had been expected in Paris **six weeks earlier**, but his arrival was delayed by a stroke.

### [[Janine Du Bois]]

Janine is a thirty-two-year-old soprano and Bonnet's former lover. They were romantically involved years earlier, particularly during time spent together in **Venice**. A strong connection remains between them.

Janine performed during the evening. Her singing carried unnaturally through the surrounding streets, reaching much farther than it reasonably should have. Bonnet secretly possesses an old sculpture of her.

### [[Louis Lépine]]

Nell used Lépine's calling card for the drawing she gave Edward. Following Nell's death, the Prefect of Police became involved in the investigation.

### [[Adrien Mercier]]

The thirty-eight-year-old sculptor studied Bonnet's work and noticed that his techniques seemed generations out of date, resembling methods from a much earlier period.

### [[Théo Marchand]]

The thirty-five-year-old painter and gossip had heard strange rumors about Bonnet's career. He suggested that Bonnet's history and artistic development contained inconsistencies.

### [[Camille Roussel]]

The forty-six-year-old art critic knew Bonnet's earlier work. Roussel said the hidden sculpture of Janine was supposedly created about ten years ago, but Bonnet **was not skilled enough at that time to have produced a work of such quality**. Judged against his known career, the sculpture should have been impossible for him to create.

## Major Clues

- Marcus possesses strange metal gears of unknown origin.
- Edward possesses a bloodstained lumberjack shirt.
- A pig at the North Star behaves strangely.
- Jackson has begun learning about occult wards.
- Breaking or disturbing a ward appears to affect Bonnet physically.
- Bonnet's hand briefly turned yellow.
- Bonnet may be unnaturally preserved or connected to death.
- Bonnet's sculpting methods appear much older than they should be.
- The Janine sculpture appears to predate Bonnet's ability to create it.
- Bonnet and Weiss have a long and possibly medical relationship.
- Bonnet had relationships with both Janine and Nell.
- Janine's voice appears to possess an unnatural quality.
- Nell knew Edward's patron.
- Nell gave Edward a drawing on Louis Lépine's calling card.
- Roberts, another North Star sculptor, is currently in Florence.
- Roberts comes from a Canadian lumber family.
- Louis Lépine announced that **Nell Whitcomb is dead**.

## Session Ending

The evening concluded with the sudden arrival of **[[Louis Lépine]]**. His announcement cut through the aftermath of the gathering:

> **Nell Whitcomb is dead.**

Session One ended there.
