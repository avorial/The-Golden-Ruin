# Henri de Toulouse-Lautrec

<div class="wiki-layout">
<div class="wiki-main">

<section class="wiki-section">
<h2>At The Table</h2>
<p>Toulouse-Lautrec sees the room from below its self-importance. He drinks hard, observes harder, and catches people at the moment when the pose begins to fail.</p>
</section>

<section class="wiki-section">
<h2>History</h2>
<p>A familiar presence in Montmartre cafés, dance halls, and cabarets, Toulouse-Lautrec has become one of the great visual chroniclers of the district. His posters of Bruant and the Moulin Rouge turn nightlife into myth while preserving its exhaustion.</p>
</section>

<section class="wiki-section">
<h2>Campaign Use</h2>
<p>He is likely to be the first famous artist the characters meet in the wild. He can provide clues through posters, barroom gossip, or drawings made while no one realized he was paying attention.</p>
</section>

## Hidden Truth

Toulouse-Lautrec has sketched a patron whose face appears differently in every drawing. He thinks it is the drink. It is not.

## GM Ideas

- His sketchbook captures a Carcosan figure standing behind the characters.
- A cabaret poster displays the Yellow Sign only after being pasted outdoors.
- He can identify which performer vanished from a composition after the fact.
:::
