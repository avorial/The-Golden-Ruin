# Charenton Asylum

## Overview

By 1895 Charenton presents itself as a humane institution for those deemed incurably insane. It can hold witnesses broken by Carcosan encounters or retired investigators overwhelmed by Shock.

## Historical Background

Charenton was a lunatic asylum founded in 1645 by the Frères de la Charité (Brothers of Charity) in Charenton-Saint-Maurice, now Saint-Maurice, Val-de-Marne, France. Charenton was first under monastic rule, then Sisters of Charity of St. Vincent de Paul took over the asylum after their founding.

This background is adapted from the [Charenton (asylum)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charenton_(asylum)) article on Wikipedia (en), accessed September 23, 2026, under the CC BY-SA license.

## Investigation Use

A patient’s impossible story becomes credible only after the same details appear in several unrelated wards.
