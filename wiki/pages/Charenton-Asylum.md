# Charenton Asylum

## Overview

By 1895 Charenton presents itself as a humane institution for those deemed incurably insane. It can hold witnesses broken by Carcosan encounters or retired investigators overwhelmed by Shock.

## Investigation Use

A patient’s impossible story becomes credible only after the same details appear in several unrelated wards.
