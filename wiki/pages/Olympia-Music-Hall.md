# Olympia Music Hall

## Overview

Its stage can accommodate circus acts as well as ballet, operetta, concerts, and variety entertainment.

## Historical Background

The Olympia opened on 12 April 1893 as Paris's first purpose-built music hall, with a capacity of roughly two thousand. Its early programs mixed acrobats, comic and cross-dressed performers, cancan, ballets, revues, operettas, pantomime, and even screenings of the Lumière brothers' films.
