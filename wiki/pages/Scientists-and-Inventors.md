# Scientists and Inventors

This parent page gathers the people most likely to test the impossible with instruments, photographs, experiments, medical observation, or stubborn skepticism.

Use this group when the characters need analysis, equipment, expert testimony, laboratory access, or the terrible comfort of knowing that an impossible phenomenon leaves measurable traces.
