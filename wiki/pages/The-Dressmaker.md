# The Dressmaker

## Overview

Claudia Martin is a gifted Latin Quarter tailor whose gowns confer beauty, confidence, and social success. They also infect their wearers with a compulsion to obey her. Dreams sent through the King in Yellow convince Martin that her work serves a higher art.

## Signs and Investigation

- Several influential women abruptly adopt identical turns of phrase or priorities.
- Wearers refuse to remove their gowns even when they are damaged or inappropriate.
- The stitching conceals repeated Carcosan forms and a fragmented Yellow Sign.
- Nightmares and missing time precede full obedience.

## Hidden Truth

Martin commands her enthralled clients and possesses physical gifts beyond an ordinary dressmaker. Freeing victims requires breaking the garment's hold as well as confronting its maker; destroying the atelier alone leaves affected clients vulnerable.
