# Mont-de-Piété

## Overview

Branches across Paris accept household goods, with reclaimed items taking days to arrive from headquarters. Mattresses are the most common pledge.

## Investigation Use

A copy of The King in Yellow enters the warehouse, or an oracular carpet answers questions through its changing weave.
