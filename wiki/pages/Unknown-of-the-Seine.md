# Unknown of the Seine

## Overview

The serene death mask of the Unknown Woman of the Seine owes its fame to Carcosan design. Every copy gives the entity another point of awareness and another route into Paris. A mask placed upon the dead can become more than a memorial.

## Investigation and Response

- Owners dream of drowning in a city with black stars.
- Copies change expression or turn toward activity in a room.
- A corpse associated with a mask moves with borrowed purpose.
- The entity's influence grows as reproductions circulate among artists and fashionable homes.

Destroying one mask only closes one window. Investigators must trace the mold, workshop, and distribution network, then identify the ritual or Carcosan agency sustaining the whole chain.
