# Marcel Proust

<div class="wiki-layout">
<div class="wiki-main">

<section class="wiki-section">
<h2>At The Table</h2>
<p>Marcel Proust is dapper, observant, socially hungry, and already collecting people as if memory were a cabinet with infinite drawers. He can flatter, pry, and wound without appearing to do any of the three.</p>
</section>

<section class="wiki-section">
<h2>History</h2>
<p>In 1895 Proust writes a society column for <em>Le Mensuel</em>. He moves through elevated company as a young social climber, traditionalist, observer, and future novelist who has not yet discovered the container large enough for everything he notices.</p>
</section>

<section class="wiki-section">
<h2>Campaign Use</h2>
<p>Proust can supply gossip, invitations, blackmail-adjacent social knowledge, and the danger of publicity. He may help the characters or turn their investigation into a whispered story before they are ready.</p>
</section>
<section class="wiki-section">
<h2>Sourcebook Profile</h2>
<ul>
<li>A dapper young society columnist for Le Mensuel who appears wherever fashionable or aristocratic people gather.</li>
<li>He can provide elite gossip, expose details investigators want hidden, or overwhelm them with snobbery.</li>
<li>Like many social climbers he is politically traditionalist and opposes efforts to separate church and state.</li>
<li>He says he will someday transform his observations of Parisian society into a novel, though he cannot imagine fitting it all into one book.</li>
</ul>
<h3>Published Scenario Uses</h3>
<ul>
<li>He is a precise observer and dangerous gossip conduit: helpful when fed the right social question, troublesome when a secret case becomes irresistible copy.</li>
</ul>
</section>

## Hidden Truth

Proust's sensitivity to memory makes him unusually vulnerable to Carcosan retrospection: memories of events that have not happened yet, or should not have happened at all.

## GM Ideas

- Proust remembers meeting one of the characters at a party that has not occurred.
- His column contains a disguised clue planted by someone else.
- He knows which aristocratic families are quietly asking about [[House of the North Star]].
:::
