# Bonepile

## Overview

A Bonepile is a heap of human remains animated by a compulsion to make itself whole. It drags loose bones into its body, rises in a clattering approximation of a person, and attacks anyone who disturbs its work.

## Signs and Investigation

- Ossuary stacks have been rearranged although no worker admits entering the passage.
- Scrape marks and trails of bone dust converge on an unrecorded chamber.
- A map of the [[Paris Catacombs]] provides an advantage in an encounter.
- The remains belong to people denied burial, identification, or peace.

## Hidden Truth

Destroying the body only scatters it. At midnight the bones begin assembling again. Permanent release requires finding the site and agency of its summoning, undoing the Yellow Sign or ritual there, and giving the dead what peace remains possible.
