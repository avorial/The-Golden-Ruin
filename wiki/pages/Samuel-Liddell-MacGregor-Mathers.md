# Samuel Liddell “MacGregor” Mathers

<div class="wiki-layout">
<div class="wiki-main">

<section class="wiki-section">
<h2>At The Table</h2>
<p>Samuel Mathers is less flamboyant in person than his reputation suggests. He can seem gray, retiring, and overburdened until authority is challenged; then the founder of an order appears.</p>
</section>

<section class="wiki-section">
<h2>History</h2>
<p>Founder of the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn, Mathers came to Paris with Moina under instructions he attributes to hidden Secret Chiefs. He researches ritual magic and Kabbalah while fighting to maintain authority over ambitious members back in London and beyond.</p>
</section>

<section class="wiki-section">
<h2>Campaign Use</h2>
<p>Mathers can provide ritual frameworks, English occult connections, and messages from questionable invisible authorities. He is helpful when respected and difficult when doubted.</p>
</section>
<section class="wiki-section">
<h2>Sourcebook Profile</h2>
<ul>
<li>Founder of the London-based Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn, he relocated to Paris with Moina four years ago on supposed instructions from the Secret Chiefs.</li>
<li>He researches ritual magic and Kabbalah while struggling to preserve authority over ambitious order members who hope to displace him.</li>
<li>English occult visitors regularly call at the couple’s villa in Auteuil.</li>
<li>Despite his order’s dramatic reputation, Samuel can seem gray, retiring, and burdened; Moina supplies the more immediate charisma.</li>
<li>The couple is nearing an Isis-centered ritual phase with white robes and leopard-fur trim.</li>
</ul>
<h3>Published Scenario Uses</h3>
<ul>
<li>Messages from the Secret Chiefs can launch investigations, while the investigators’ Carcosan discoveries may trigger the couple’s new Isis rites and worsen Samuel’s struggle for authority.</li>
</ul>
</section>

## Hidden Truth

One message Mathers attributes to the Secret Chiefs did not come from any source he recognizes. It names a pallid court and urges an Isis rite at the wrong hour.

## GM Ideas

- Mathers asks the PCs to recover a stolen cipher manuscript from a Paris occult rival.
- A Golden Dawn ritual protects against one manifestation but opens another channel.
- His authority crisis worsens when Carcosan instructions produce visible results.
:::
