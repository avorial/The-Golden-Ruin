# Winter Worm

## Overview

The Winter Worm poses as a priest or healer. Through a psychic bond it removes hunger and pain, earning worship from sick or desperate hosts while their bodies waste away in bliss. Its pale, scarf-like adult form carries egg sacs along its spine.

## Investigation and Response

- The healed stop eating and praise their declining health.
- A pale “scarf” is seen moving independently around the healer's shoulders.
- Egg sacs hatch into thousands of tiny worms that seek new hosts.
- A Difficulty 4 First Aid test can remove a worm; resistance increases the difficulty by 2.

Break the community's belief and psychic dependency, then destroy both the parent and its offspring. Leaving either side intact allows the infestation to begin again.
