# The King in Yellow RPG — GM Primer & Checklist

> GM-side reference for this system: the concept, the reformatting checklist, and the lists you'll reach for most.

## Concept

The King in Yellow RPG demos should be structured around contamination by art, altered timelines, and obsessive clues. The sheet should support identities that may change across sequence, shock/injury pressure, relationships, and the cursed text at the center.

## Sheet Field Groups To Review

- Identity
- Occupation
- Drive
- Investigative Abilities
- General Abilities
- Shock
- Injury
- Contacts
- Clues
- Cursed Media
- Continuity Notes

## Visual Direction Notes

- case-file styling
- evidence boxes
- sanity/stability tracks
- relationship clues

## Cleanup Checklist

- Differentiate clue mechanics from general skills.
- Avoid copying official case-sheet typography or seal graphics.

## Yellow Sign Clues

- Playbill
- Mask
- Unstageable line
- Audience memory
- Impossible review
- Set design sketch
- Actor disappearance

## Scene Modes

- Bohemian party
- Police inquiry
- Theatre rehearsal
- Hospital recovery
- Unreliable city
- Future echo

## Sheet UI Priorities

- Case-era identity
- Shock/injury tracks
- Ability pools
- Cursed media notes
- Continuity/change log
