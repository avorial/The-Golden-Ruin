# Père-Lachaise Cemetery

## Overview

Molière, Chopin, Balzac, Delacroix, Héloïse and Abelard, and the heart of Jacques-Louis David rest here, at least officially. Older remains were frequently moved and mislabeled, while a massive crematory furnace smokes among the monuments.

## Historical Background

Opened in 1804 as the Cemetery of the East, Père Lachaise was Paris's first municipal garden cemetery. Transfers of the reputed remains of Molière, La Fontaine, Héloïse, and Abélard helped make it fashionable; by 1830 it held more than 33,000 graves. The Communards' Wall marks the execution of 147 prisoners during Bloody Week in 1871.
