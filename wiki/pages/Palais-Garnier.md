# Palais Garnier

## Overview

Construction was interrupted by the Siege and Commune, when the unfinished building served as a fortress. Open since 1875, it contains grand stairs, velvet halls, and cisterns controlling water that invaded the foundations.

## Investigation Use

The rumored underground lake is exaggerated, but something moves through the real cistern system.
