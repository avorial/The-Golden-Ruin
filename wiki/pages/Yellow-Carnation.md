# Yellow Carnation

## Overview

A florist near the Palais Bourbon launches a yellow-carnation fashion using Carcosan seed. On a susceptible wearer, the flower enters through the chest, encourages escalating indulgence, and grows roots throughout the body. After roughly two weeks it controls the host and begins infecting the host's circle.

## Investigation and Response

- Victims adopt the same flower shortly before abrupt changes in appetite and behavior.
- Fine yellow roots show beneath the skin or in blood.
- Social groups spread the fad faster than an ordinary shop could supply it.
- A Natural History Push can formulate an antidote, administered with Difficulty 4 First Aid.

Stopping the outbreak requires treating current hosts, closing the shop, destroying seed stock, and tracing the Carcosan supplier.
