# Church of St. Barnabé

## Overview

The church adapts the Saint-Roch setting used in “In the Court of the Dragon.” Music and polished ornament mask the possibility of a Yellow King apparition.

## Investigation Use

An organ performance traps one listener inside the vision described by Chambers.
