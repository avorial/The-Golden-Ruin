# Paris Morgue

## Overview

Freezing machinery preserves corpses for months or years. About nine hundred unidentified bodies arrive annually; photographers record each, and coroners have autopsy and toxicology facilities.

## Investigation Use

A body changes between its photograph and the public viewing table, or remains unclaimed because every potential relative remembers a different face.
