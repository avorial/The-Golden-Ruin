# Paris Morgue

## Overview

Freezing machinery preserves corpses for months or years. About nine hundred unidentified bodies arrive annually; photographers record each, and coroners have autopsy and toxicology facilities.

## Historical Background

The public morgue stood on the Quai de l'Archevêché near Notre-Dame. Unidentified bodies, especially those recovered from the Seine, were displayed behind glass with their clothing so visitors could identify them. Macabre crowds made it a spectacle, but the method was effective: contemporary accounts credited it with identifying roughly three quarters of bodies found in the river.
