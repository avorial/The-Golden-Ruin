# Nokk

## Overview

A Nokk is a fish, crustacean, and humanoid predator associated with corrupted waters near the [[Jardin des Plantes]]. By plucking its dorsal spines it produces an enchanting music and a perceptual glamour that makes witnesses accept it as human.

## Investigation and Response

- Music comes from water after the gardens close.
- Victims vanish near ponds and return only as gnawed remains.
- Water lilies, knives, fresh meat, and the creature's distance from water all affect an encounter.
- Prolonged life on land slowly makes it more mammalian but also weakens it.

Its song, not beauty, is the center of the glamour. Silencing it and forcing it away from water strips away its strongest advantages.
