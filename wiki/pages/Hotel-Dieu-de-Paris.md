# Hôtel-Dieu de Paris

## Overview

Founded in 651, its constant bustle can make even a demanding patient anonymous. Injured investigators may appreciate interns too busy to question strange wounds.

## Investigation Use

The anonymity that protects the characters also allows someone else to exchange a patient or medical chart.
