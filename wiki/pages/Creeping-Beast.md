# Creeping Beast

## Overview

The beast begins as a leopard in a post-impressionist canvas. Only its chosen victim notices that it looks back. Soon it appears in mirrors, photographs, advertisements, and unrelated paintings, edging closer each time.

## Signs and Investigation

- Witnesses insist an animal in a gallery painting changed position.
- The same leopard appears in images made before the painting existed.
- Reflections show claw marks or movement absent from the room itself.
- Threatening the original canvas can force the predator to reveal itself.

## Hidden Truth

The creature crosses from image to reality to maul its quarry while remaining almost impossible to see directly. Severing its supernatural link to the original work, or destroying that link under controlled conditions, is safer than waiting for its final manifestation.
